## 1. Executive classification

The repository has substantial cognitive, runtime, identity, authorization, agent, research-acquisition, cyber-range, event-fusion, evaluation and global-fabric primitives. However, several large product planes are currently represented primarily by architecture documents and interfaces rather than identifiable end-to-end executable systems.

Most important classification:

- **NEXUS:** there are small evidence/domain assessment structures, but the inspected tree contains no dedicated NEXUS semantic graph/protocol implementation path.
- **FRL:** formal reasoning and proof architecture is documented; the inspected tree does not expose a dedicated formal kernel/checker package or proof-artifact registry.
- **MIRROR WORLD:** architecture is extensively specified; the inspected core contains a bounded synthetic cyber range and FINCH event/forecast primitives, but no dedicated integrated world-model/simulation orchestration package was identified.
- **Systems Reverse Engineering:** authorized cyber simulation exists for synthetic sandbox targets; a general authorized software/protocol/system understanding pipeline was not identified as an end-to-end implementation.
- **W10 domains:** UniversalIntelligenceFabric provides bounded keyword routing and Evidence/DomainAssessment structures; this is not a registry of independently qualified specialists across all declared domains.
- **W12 business/community:** business/project lifecycle and finance/community boundaries are documented, but the inspected source tree does not expose dedicated payment, escrow, marketplace, double-entry ledger or independent impact measurement implementation modules.
- **W11 global operations:** local global fabric/scale primitives exist; fleet-wide durable orchestration, capacity leases, SLO/DR and release chain remain architectural requirements.

These classifications describe the inspected repository tree and exact source paths. A separately deployed or externally connected service may exist, but it is not established by this repository evidence.

### 4.2 Required MIRROR WORLD implementation architecture

The architecture must separately specify:
- immutable WorldManifest, domain, model/kernel and dataset manifests;
- source/observation ingestion and sensor identity;
- normalized units, dimensions, coordinates, time and uncertainty;
- initial state, interventions, causal assumptions and experiment specification;
- domain kernels and fidelity F0–F5;
- deterministic execution, checkpoints, reproducibility and resource budgets;
- independent calibration/validation, temporal holdouts and OOD handling;
- synthetic-web, cyber-range, physical, environmental, social and economic trust zones;
- simulation artifact and source-to-output lineage;
- non-actuating Reality Bridge with a separate human/authorized action gate.

### 4.3 Systems Reverse Engineering scope

Keep the following modes distinct and explicitly authorized:

- passive public documentation and standards analysis;
- user-owned source/build/artifact analysis;
- static binary/protocol/format inspection under a declared asset authorization;
- controlled lab or synthetic target experiments;
- active network testing only with explicit owner scope, rate limits, stop conditions and isolated evidence custody.

The cyber-range implementation currently supports synthetic sandbox targets, not all these modes. Do not describe it as a general reverse-engineering engine.

## 5. W10 — Domain intelligence realization

`UniversalIntelligenceFabric` contains a finite set of domains (software engineering, architecture engineering, legal intelligence, medical evidence, scientific research, finance/economics and general reasoning) and keyword matching. Evidence and DomainAssessment are useful typed foundations.

Still required per domain:
- independent specialist/organization identity and qualification evidence;
- scope, jurisdiction, credential source, expiry and revocation;
- standards, accepted source classes, methods and units;
- calibration/validation cohort and uncertainty limits;
- conflict-of-interest disclosure and reviewer independence;
- prohibited outputs/actions and human escalation;
- evidence retention, correction and appeal.

A route label, agent personality, domain name or self-declared skill is not professional qualification.

## 6. W11 — Global operations realization

Identifiable code paths include `ecosystem/global_fabric.py`, `ecosystem/global_scale.py`, `core/resource_router.py`, `core/observability.py`, `core/alerting.py`, `core/secure_boot.py` and `core/supply_chain.py`.

They provide bounded local abstractions and primitives. The architecture must still specify and later prove:
- distributed node/region/cell membership and attestation;
- durable capacity reservation, expiring lease and fencing;
- residency and jurisdiction policy;
- replicated durable event/memory stores and acknowledgement/quorum;
- service objectives, metering, fairness, backpressure and cost/energy accounting;
- disaster recovery, backup/restore, incident ownership and drills;
- artifact→builder→signer→approval→deployment→rollback provenance.

Local in-memory registries, deterministic placement and digest checks do not establish global fleet operations.

## 7. W12 — Business, marketplace, community and impact realization

`architecture/BUSINESS_PROJECT_FABRIC.md` is an architecture document, not an executable ledger. The inspected source tree contains no dedicated named modules for payment processing, escrow, marketplace settlement, double-entry ledger, restricted funds or independent environmental impact measurement.

Architecture boundaries already defined must be preserved:
- models and agents may calculate/propose, never move funds directly;
- user funds, third-party funds, company revenue, community contributions and restricted grants remain segregated;
- transactions require explicit consent, beneficiary/purpose checks, limits, idempotency and an authorized payment provider;
- ledger entries require double-entry accounting, settlement reconciliation, refunds/chargebacks, dispute and audit;
- community governance needs membership, transparent budgets, worker safeguarding, conflict declarations, appeal and exit rights;
- environmental impact distinguishes activity, output, outcome and causally supported net impact, with baseline, boundary, uncertainty and independent measurement.

These are planned architecture boundaries; no live financial or environmental service is claimed by this source inventory.
