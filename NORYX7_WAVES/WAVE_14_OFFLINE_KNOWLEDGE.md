# WAVE 14 — OFFLINE & KNOWLEDGE FABRIC

Dependency: Wave 13 CLOSED.

## Goal
Connect offline core, research, knowledge retrieval and online web research into one evidence/provenance model.

## Must verify
- offline mode never silently claims live information;
- online fallback is explicit and policy-controlled;
- source relevance is verified before admission;
- stale data carries freshness metadata;
- annotations and research artifacts bind to execution/user context;
- no network access bypasses the egress boundary;
- evidence is separated from model-generated claims.

## Closure tests
Offline arithmetic/knowledge task; offline research unavailable; online source mismatch; stale source; network denial; source injection; evidence provenance; online/offline semantic parity where applicable.
